Sami

The engine on this site, with an interface on it. Compression and conversion that runs on your device — nothing uploaded, nothing queued on somebody else’s server, no watermark on the way out.

01 — Why it costs money

Because the alternative is your files on somebody else’s computer

Every free online compressor is paid for by something. Usually it is your media sitting on a server you cannot inspect, for a retention period you did not agree to, processed by a queue you are last in.

Sami runs the encoders on the device you are holding, using the hardware that is already in it. There is no upload step because there is nowhere to upload to.

Lathe is the engine, and it is free. Sami is the product.

02 — Version 2

What is coming

The engine grew a long way past compression. Version 2 is that work reaching the interface.

Metadata, properly

Cover artwork and episode data pulled from TMDb with your own API key, written into the fields Apple players actually read — stik is what decides Movies from TV Shows, and EXIF is not used by video at all.

LatheMeta · LatheLookup

Fix a mismatch

Filename parsing gets it right most of the time and wrong some of the time. A search box and a list of candidates is the difference between a tool you trust and one you check afterwards.

MediaTitleParser

Subtitles, muxed in

Fetched from OpenSubtitles and written as a real subtitle track rather than burned into the picture, so they can still be turned off.

tx3g · OpenSubtitlesProvider

Audio and chapters

AAC, ALAC, Opus, FLAC and MP3, with loudness probing and channel policy. Chapters survive a transcode, in video and audio both, which is rarer than it should be.

LatheAudio · LatheMP3

PDFs and comics

Reorder, insert, remove and merge pages across PDF and CBZ. Reordering a comic renames its pages zero-padded, because its readers sort by filename and a reshuffle nobody can see is not a reorder.

LatheDoc

Video into stills

A strip of frames out of any video — a set number spread across it, or one every so many seconds. Sampled from the middle of each slice, because the first and last frames of a video are usually black and a contact sheet should not open and close on them.

FrameExtractor.frames

Video into audio

The soundtrack, on its own. The picture is never decoded — only the audio track is read and re-encoded — which is why it finishes about as fast as the file can be read.

AudioTranscoder

Metadata-only mode

Change the tags and leave the bytes alone. No re-encode, no generation loss, no waiting — for the very common case where the file is fine and only its labels are wrong.

MetadataWriter
03 — Together

Lathe downloads it. Sami makes it smaller.

The free Mac app is a downloader. Turn on the hand-off and anything it finishes goes straight to Sami for conversion or compression under whatever preset you set, into a destination you chose.

Neither app needs the other. Together they are a pipeline that never touches a server.

The hand-off

  • Pick a download folder once
  • Choose compression or conversion, and a preset
  • Lathe sends the request with the media, as one delivery
  • Sami picks it up and gets to work
