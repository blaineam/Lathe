Why it costs money

Because the alternative is your files on someone else's computer

Every free online compressor is paid for by something. Usually it is your media on a server you cannot inspect, for a retention period you did not agree to, in a queue you are last in.

Sami runs the encoders on your own device, on the hardware already in it. There is no upload step because there is nowhere to upload to.

Lathe is the engine, and it is free. Sami is the product.
